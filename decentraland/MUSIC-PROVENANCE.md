# Original procedural soundtrack

## Current v0.8 interactive arrangement

`scripts/generate-concert-feedback.mjs` creates six percussion-only `bed-0.wav` through `bed-5.wav` files (7 seconds each), three C/E/G playable `lead-0.wav` through `lead-2.wav` notes (0.55 seconds each), and `mistake.wav` (0.19 seconds). All are original deterministic synthesis, mono 22050 Hz 16-bit PCM. Regenerate with `pnpm audio:feedback`. The percussion has a downbeat at the first note target; the lead plays only on locally timed correct input. Wrong and missed notes trigger the short dissonant cue, not a continuing prerecorded lead. Authoritative scores remain server-peer-confirmed, so local musical feedback is not a score receipt.

The first deliberate studio-instrument interaction normally enables sound. An explicit mute choice is preserved. Idle arrival is silent; Menu exposes mute. Live beds begin three seconds into a round, aligning their two-second lead-in with the five-second first live target. The result chord now marks a scored result, not HP damage. Native decode, touch latency, volume balance and audio feel remain unverified until physical-device testing. PCM and routing tests are not listening tests.

## Historical v0.5–v0.7 soundtrack

Also bundled: `resolve.wav`, a 0.65-second original synthesized C-major chord (28710 bytes), played only after confirmed HP loss. It is generated by the same script, with no imported sample.

Generated for Affinity Arena on September 8, 2026 by the included deterministic `scripts/generate-arena-audio.mjs`. No external recording, sample, lyrics, artist voice, downloaded melody or music-streaming service was used. Each track is a short synthesized exercise, not a licensed commercial song or a representation of every tradition within a genre.
