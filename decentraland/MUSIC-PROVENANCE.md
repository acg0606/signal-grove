# Original procedural soundtrack

Also bundled: `resolve.wav`, a 0.65-second original synthesized C-major chord (28710 bytes), played only after confirmed HP loss. It is generated by the same script, with no imported sample.

Generated for Affinity Arena on September 8, 2026 by the included deterministic `scripts/generate-arena-audio.mjs`. No external recording, sample, lyrics, artist voice, downloaded melody or music-streaming service was used. Each track is a short synthesized exercise, not a licensed commercial song or a representation of every tradition within a genre.

All six tracks last seven seconds, mono PCM, 22050 Hz, 16-bit, 308744 bytes each (about 1.85 MB total). Regenerate using `pnpm audio:generate`; the seed and waveforms are in source. Audio starts only after the visitor enables it, at 40% component volume, and can be muted. Gameplay remains visually signalled. This is a provenance statement, not a legal guarantee of exclusivity or public-domain status.

We intentionally did not use an existing recording of an old composition: composition and recording have separate rights. Reference checked September 8, 2026: https://www.copyright.gov/register/pa-sr.html . No claim is made that speeding up someone else's recording removes its copyright.

SDK source `PBAudioSource` 7.27.0 supports global playback and currentTime. WAV support is documented at https://docs.decentraland.org/creator/content-creator-pt/scenes-sdk7/essenciais-de-conteudo-3d/sounds . Actual mobile decode, audible synchronization and perceived quality remain NOT RUN until a supported client is available. Automated PCM checks are not listening tests.
