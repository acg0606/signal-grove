## Rules contract

Matchmaking lasts 20 seconds. A visitor can cancel. Empty seats become labeled bots only in an exhibition; human-only matches remain distinct. Rehearsal and live attributes are rhythm, precision, harmony and consistency, normalized 0–100. Final attributes = (rehearsal + 2 × live) / 3. Sum of four final attributes decides victory; equal sums are draws. The NPC crowd visualizes that result; it is not a poll of real visitors.
