What this slice tests

One mechanic, four product questions.

01

Can social intent be legible?

Players see companion signals before acting, turning coordination into a readable decision instead of hidden luck.

02

Can touch feel intentional?

Three large actions, concise language and immediate contrast make the core loop usable on narrow screens.

03

Can cooperation create mastery?

The strongest move is not always the same action. It is the action that expands the group's current range.

04

Can evidence stay honest?

Every replay proof says local demo, no connection and scripted companions. It cannot masquerade as a live world session.
